Ask a security question (RAG over the knowledge graph)

POST /api/soc/ask
Template-based GraphRAG: your question is matched to a Cypher query against Neo4j, and the answer is generated directly from the graph results — no LLM involved. Try: "Why was this alert created?", "What controls mitigate credential attacks?", "Which services are affected if resource-service fails?", "Which users are linked to repeated failed logins?"
